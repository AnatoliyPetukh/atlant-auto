Why can an auction vehicle cost less?

It is a professional wholesale market where ex-lease and fleet vehicles are common. We assess any saving only after comparing the vehicle price, fees, transport and preparation.

Am I buying the car blind?

No. Before you decide, we show the photos, seller description, available reports, service records and known notes. The scope depends on the platform and the specific vehicle.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on the country, seller and carrier. We confirm loading and estimated arrival after purchase.

Can the car be registered in Poland?

Yes. We check the document set and help with the required stages of vehicle registration in Poland.

Which documents will I receive?

We provide the seller and purchase documents, available vehicle documents and the materials needed for the next formalities. The exact set depends on the country and vehicle source.

What is charged separately from the service fee?

The vehicle price, seller or auction fees, paid reports, transport, registration, insurance and any repairs are agreed separately.

When is payment for the vehicle due?

We explain the payment process and deadlines before bidding. The purchase stays within your approved limit, and payments are tied to the documents for the specific transaction.

Can a technical inspection be arranged?

Whether a pre-purchase inspection is possible depends on the platform. Our extended package includes arranging a standard post-purchase check at an agreed workshop.
