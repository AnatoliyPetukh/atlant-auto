Process

How the purchase works

We first agree your requirements and budget, then present checked options with a clear estimate. You decide after reviewing the vehicle, documents and total cost.

  1. 01Requirements and budget

    Make, model, mileage, timing, purchase country and maximum budget.

  2. 02Sourcing and checks

    We find listings, check history and calculate fees, delivery and paperwork.

  3. 03Purchase

    We bid or negotiate with the dealer within the agreed limit.

  4. 04Delivery and documents

    We deliver to Warsaw, receive the vehicle and prepare registration or export documents.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why can an auction vehicle cost less?

They are often ex-lease or fleet vehicles sold without a retail mark-up.

Am I buying the car blind?

Before bidding, you receive photos, a description, available reports, service history and a total estimate.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on the country, seller and carrier. We confirm loading and estimated arrival after purchase.

Can the car be registered in Poland?

Yes. We can manage turnkey registration or prepare an export document package.
