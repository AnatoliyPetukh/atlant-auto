Agreement, budget and payment

Before making a financial decision, the client receives the contracting party details, agreed scope and cost structure for the specific transaction.

01

Contracting party

Terms are confirmed with Atlant Capital Sp. z o.o.; registration details are published on the site and can be checked in KRS.

02

Payment against documentation

The amount, payment reference and recipient details are set out in the document for the specific transaction.

03

Itemised costs

Vehicle price, service, transport and additional formalities are shown separately before approval.

04

Client approval

The purchase proceeds only within the agreed limit after the vehicle and known costs have been approved.

A transparent process based on available evidence

Every stage is agreed with the client. Before any financial decision, you receive vehicle information and a clear cost breakdown. We do not publish invented ratings or guarantees. The scope of checks and the estimate depend on the selected vehicle.
